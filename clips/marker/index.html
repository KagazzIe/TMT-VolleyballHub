Angles and sync

Clips are marked once for every angle. To line an angle up, pause on a sharp moment, like a serve contact or a whistle, then nudge that angle or type its offset until it shows the same moment. A positive offset means the video started later than the first one. The first angle is the one clips open on, with its sound.
