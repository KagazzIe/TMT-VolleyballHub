S setter OH outside MB middle OPP opposite L libero (never serves)

Overlap rule: when the ball is served, each back-row player stays behind their front-row partner (1 behind 2, 6 behind 3, 5 behind 4), and neighbors stay in order left to right.

Reading the hitter: hold base until the ball is set, then move. Count the rhythm: pass, set, hit. If you haven't seen the ball by "3," expect a tip.

Position ideas adapted from Stork Hsu's 5-1 positional defense diagrams (2003). Shaded areas are dig zones and they overlap where defenders share a ball. The setter covers only a small piece of court.
