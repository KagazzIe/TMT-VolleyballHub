Team setup

Fill this in, then save or download your team.json file. It sets your team's name on the hub, and the players and play labels in Clip Marker.

Filled in from your team's current file.

Shown at the top of your hub.
One name per line, in the order you want them in Clip Marker. The first players get number keys 1, 2, 3 and so on.
The kinds of plays you tag in Clip Marker, one per line, in the order you want them listed. Add your own, like Dig or Free ball. Renaming a label later doesn't rename clips you already tagged.
Extra labels for clips, one per line, like Good or Bad. A clip can have several. Clip Marker only offers the tags listed here, so everyone uses the same ones. Optional.

  1. Open your team's repository on GitHub.
  2. Click Add file, then Upload files.
  3. Drag in team.json. It replaces the old one if there is one.
  4. Click Commit changes. Your site updates in about two minutes.
